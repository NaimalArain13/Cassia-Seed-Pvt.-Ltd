About Us
Who We Are
We are a leading seed company specializing in research, trade, and marketing of high-quality
hybrid and open pollination seeds. Our main goal is to empower farmers with advanced,

sustainable farming. Explores our wide range of hybrid and open pollinated vegetable seeds,

Cassia Seed Private Limited is a professional research, trade, and marketing company
dedicated to improving agricultural productivity. We focus on the research and development
of superior hybrid and open pollinations vegetable seed varieties that meet the needs of
modern farming and ensure consistent growth.
What We Do
Research & Development (R&D): Creating high yielding, disease resistant, and climate
tolerant seed varieties.
Seed Trading: Importing, local seed producing and distributing premium quality hybrid and
open pollination vegetable seeds to farmers and dealers.
Marketing & Support: We conduct farmer meetings and provide expert guidance, field
demonstrations, and marketing support to ensure farmers benefit from our innovations.
Our Commitment
We are committed to delivering seeds that farmers can trust, with advanced research
facilities, strict quality control, and strong distribution networks, we help farmers increase
productivity and profitability.

History
The foundation of Seed & Solace (Growth with comfort and hope) formally, CASSIA SEED
PRIVATE LIMITED is built on more than a decade of hands-on experience and a relentless
commitment to Pakistan’s agricultural sector.
Our journey began in 1985. It wasn't a corporate launch, but it was a humble, local effort
starting with a small agricultural seed shop.
For forty intense years, he poured his dedication into this small business. He didn't just sell
seeds, but he built trust. Through hard work, deep market knowledge, and an unwavering
focus on quality, the humble shop grew its reputation exponentially, evolving from a local
dealer into a recognized, reliable supplier and distributor across the region.
This success confirmed the need for a larger, more structured entity. In 2022, that personal
vision transformed into the formal establishment of Cassia Seed Private Limited. This
marked a pivotal moment, leading in a new chapter focused on scaling impact. Today, the
company operates as a key importer and local producer of high-quality vegetable seeds. We
are dedicated to leveraging global expertise and local production capabilities, ensuring to
continue driving technological adoption and sustainable growth of Pakistan’s agriculture
sector.
